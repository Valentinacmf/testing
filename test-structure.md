# Amazon Title Test — Positioning Pillar Study

## Method
Ad → fake PDP. Each of 6 positioning pillars is tested as a self-contained message: paid ad creative drives traffic to a matching Amazon-style listing page. The PDP is mocked (no real purchase); the click on **Add to Cart** is the conversion event.

## Variables under test
Everything else is held constant. Only these three change between cells:

1. **Title** — one word/phrase that anchors the pillar
2. **Subtitle below CTA** — one-sentence value prop framing
3. **Star rating + review count + Amazon badge** — calibrated to fit the pillar's vibe (premium = high stars + bestseller; value = lower stars + huge volume; etc.)

Held constant: image, breadcrumb, price (`$$`), Prime delivery line, button color/copy, search bar (blank).

## The 6 pillars

| Pillar | Angle | Title | Vibe |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Social Proof & Premium | Expert-Endorsed | top-rated, IBCLC-approved |
| B | Proactive Care & Science-Backed | Daily Wellness | preventative, evidence-based |
| C | Freedom & Control | Hands-Free | working mom, supply protection |
| D | Simplicity & Passive Relief | Effortless | works while you mom |
| E | Clinical Credibility | Therapeutic | problem-specific, targeted |
| F | Value & Access | Just Comfortable | affordable, no fuss |

## Ad creative pairing
Each pillar gets 1 static + 1 short-form video ad on Meta and a matching Google Search ad. Ad copy uses the same pillar language so the landing page feels like a continuation, not a switch. Same hero image as the PDP for visual continuity.

## Metrics

**Primary:** Add-to-Cart click rate on the fake PDP (intent proxy)
**Secondary:** Ad CTR, time-on-page, scroll depth to subtitle
**Qualitative:** Optional exit micro-survey ("what made this feel right for you?")

## Sample size
~1,000 ad clicks per cell to detect a ~3pt difference in add-to-cart rate at 80% power. 6 cells × 1,000 = ~6,000 clicks total. At ~$1.50 CPC, budget ~$9k.

## Read-out
Rank pillars by add-to-cart rate. Pull the top 2 into a follow-up moderated test to dig into the "why" before committing copy direction.
